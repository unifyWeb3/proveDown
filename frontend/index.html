What is real, derived, synthetic, or mocked?

✓ REAL Studio contract reads, finalized attestation records, evidence hashes, reputation readback, and the full Explorer references above.

◆ DERIVED Confidence bars, observed-versus-required comparisons, case labels, and the plain-language summaries are views of returned data.

Protocol detail

The contract stores a resolved, inconclusive, or no_consensus status. A result is presented as definitive only when the stored record is readable; this page does not create or approve a new transaction.

ProveDown is a functional-quality attestation surface, not uptime monitoring. Current Worker evidence is synthetic and downstream settlement remains mocked.
