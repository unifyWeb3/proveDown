What is real, derived, synthetic, or mocked?

✓ REAL Studio contract reads, finalized attestation records, evidence hashes, reputation readback, and the full Explorer references above.

◆ DERIVED Confidence bars, observed-versus-required comparisons, case labels, and plain-language summaries are views of returned data.

○ SYNTHETIC / MOCK Worker bundles are deterministic fixtures. Base/Hyperlane relay, future polling, and any SSE fallback are mocked and never represented as finalized.

Protocol detail
