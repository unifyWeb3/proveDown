Why not just uptime monitoring?

Uptime checks factual reachability (GET 200 → UP). ProveDown checks the functional obligation: the same HTTP 200 with fill 72% vs required 80% → BREACH, with evidence hash, confidence, breach reason and a reliability record — not just up/down.
